# The ultraviolet LEDs, first light

**Date:** 2026-10-02. **Software:** 0.1.0.dev0 (commit `1827041`), through the viewer
started with `--allow-uv`. **Device:** one HC1500 (`hc1500-1`). The operator confirmed
beforehand: lid of the dark box closed, USB2 (UV supply) connected, nobody able to see
the light. **In view:** a small circuit board on white office paper, about 30 cm away.

Before any UV LED was lit, "Check" confirmed the box was dark (black level 5, 5, 5) and
the camera paired. Each UV LED was lit once for the scan (about 0.6-0.7 s); LED 3 was
lit once more for 1.3 s as a first test. Afterwards every LED was off and the picture
back at 5, 5, 5.

## What was measured

A scan of all 17 channels completed in 18.7 s without a warning; every channel was
selected at the first attempt. The UV channels used the model's PWM values. Mean of the
frame, dark subtracted:

| LED | nm | PWM | on the paper: R / G / B | on the circuit board: R / G / B |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 255 | 458 | 7.1 / 6.9 / 34.5 | 0.4 / 0.1 / 1.8 |
| 2 | 275 | 300 | 6.7 / 6.3 / 32.5 | 0.4 / 0.1 / 1.4 |
| 3 | 370 | 74 | 13.7 / 14.4 / 67.2 | 0.8 / 0.6 / 3.6 |
| 4 | 400 | 74 | 6.8 / 5.8 / 33.5 | 0.3 / -0.5 / 2.4 |
| 5 (for comparison) | 455 | 96 | 16.9 / 20.3 / 140.5 | 7.7 / 6.9 / 36.7 |

## Conclusions

1. **All four UV LEDs light, and the UV gate does what it says**: without `--allow-uv`
   the same device refuses them (the viewer showed "UV locked" all day).
2. **On office paper the four UV bands show the same picture in the same colour**
   (red : green : blue about 0.2 : 0.2 : 1 under 255, 275, 370 and 400 nm alike), and
   the circuit board stays almost black. That is what fluorescence of the paper's
   optical brighteners looks like: the camera sees the blue light the paper gives off,
   not reflected ultraviolet. It supports what [hardware.md](../hardware.md) says about
   LEDs 1 and 2. A target that does not fluoresce, and a filter, are needed to say what
   the camera sees of the ultraviolet itself.
3. **A scan of all 17 channels takes about 19 s** on this computer.
4. Office paper cannot serve as a white reference for LEDs 1-4: dividing by it would
   divide by its fluorescence.

## Later the same day: with USB2 unplugged

The operator confirmed again: lid closed, nobody able to see the light. USB2 was
unplugged. "Check" first (black level 5, 5, 5), then one scan of LEDs 1-5, same scene,
same PWM values, same camera parameters read back. Mean of the frame, dark subtracted:

| LED | nm | USB2 connected: R / G / B | USB2 unplugged: R / G / B |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 255 | 6.2 / 5.7 / 29.6 | 16.3 / 34.9 / 137.9 |
| 2 | 275 | 5.7 / 5.0 / 26.7 | 16.3 / 33.3 / 133.4 |
| 3 | 370 | 11.5 / 11.9 / 56.8 | 29.2 / 51.1 / 163.9 |
| 4 | 400 | 5.5 / 4.5 / 26.1 | 27.7 / 25.9 / 131.7 |
| 5 | 455 | 16.8 / 18.7 / 125.9 | 41.3 / 74.0 / 235.0 (clipping) |

5. **All four UV LEDs light without USB2.** An unplugged USB2 is no safeguard; the
   documents no longer say it is.
6. **Everything was several times brighter than before.** LED 9 at PWM 144 gave a frame
   mean of 250 (93 % of the pixels clipped) where it had given 141; a quarter of the
   exposure brought it back to 138. Between the two measurements USB2 was unplugged and
   the camera cable was pulled and plugged twice, so either the light source is driven
   harder without USB2 or the camera came back with another gain than it reports. Not
   settled: USB2 has to be plugged in again to tell. Until then a white reference is
   only good for scans made in the same state.

   **Settled the same afternoon:** it is the camera. It keeps the gain its automatic
   exposure had chosen when the parameters were locked, and reports none of it. See
   [2026-10-02-hidden-camera-gain.md](2026-10-02-hidden-camera-gain.md). The right-hand
   column above is therefore brighter because of the camera, not because of USB2.
